About

An independent, open scoreboard that grades AI-skill security scanners. We don't sell a scanner. Methodology pre-registered; corpus private (anti-gaming); aggregate results public. Built by a solo white-hat.

Not a certification. We grade scanners, not skills. Results describe specific pinned versions on a dated, directional corpus and may not generalise. No warranty; not security advice.

Scanner names are trademarks of their owners, used nominatively to identify the tools tested. No affiliation or endorsement; no vendor logos. Vendor neutrality: no preferential treatment, no right of reply, no methodology accommodation; a vendor note may be posted at our discretion and never alters scoring. Spot a factual/method error? Corrections welcome from anyone.
