About

An independent, open scoreboard that grades AI-skill security scanners. We grade scanners, not skills, and we sell no scanner. Methodology pre-registered; corpus private (anti-gaming); aggregate results public. Built by a solo white-hat.

Independence & disclosure

This scoreboard sells nothing and takes no vendor money. Disclosure: the author also maintains open-source skill-security tooling (the skillscan-* projects, including a static scanner and an ML detector). None of the author's own tools are graded in this benchmark, none are sold commercially, and the winning approach here (LLM semantic reading) is reported as an architecture finding, not a product endorsement. Corrections welcome from anyone.
